Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Media Swipe: Gallery Cleaner ("the App", "we", "our") is developed and operated by an independent developer, Cypher (contact: Cypher.deve@gmail.com). This policy explains what data the App accesses, what we do (and don't do) with it, and your choices.

1. Data We Access

The App requests permission to access your device's photo and video library (gallery/camera roll) solely to let you view, sort, and manage your own media. This includes:

  • Photos and videos stored on your device
  • Basic file metadata (creation date, file size) used to sort and calculate storage space

2. On-Device Processing Only

All photo and video review, duplicate detection, and cleanup actions happen entirely on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded, transmitted, copied to, or stored on any external server, and we do not have access to them. Nothing you swipe, delete, or keep leaves your phone.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

To remember your progress, the App saves a small amount of data locally on your device only: your settings (theme, sound and haptic preferences), your swipe progress and decisions (such as which items you have kept, marked for deletion, or set aside for comparison), and notification timing information. This data stays on your device and is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

4. Advertising

The App displays ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device and usage data (such as advertising ID, IP address, device type, and general usage statistics) to serve and measure ads. This data collection is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's own privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region where consent is required, the App shows a consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → "Ad privacy choices". Everywhere, you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device's ad settings (Settings → Google → Ads on Android).

5. Notifications

The App may send local, on-device notifications (e.g. reminders to clean up your gallery). A periodic background check on your device decides whether a reminder is useful, based on information such as your storage usage and how many new photos have been added. This check and the notifications themselves run entirely on your device and are not tied to any external service or account. You can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.

6. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require account creation, sign-in, or any personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • We do not upload or store your photos, videos, or file contents anywhere
  • We do not sell your data. The only data shared with a third party is the ad-serving data collected by Google AdMob, as described above

7. Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions:

  • Photo/Media access — required for the App's core gallery-cleaning function
  • Notifications — used for optional cleanup reminders; can be disabled in your device settings at any time

You can revoke any permission at any time via your device's Settings app. If photo access is revoked, the App will be unable to function until it is granted again.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). Ads shown in the App are served by Google AdMob under its own policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help resolve it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: cypher.deve@gmail.com
